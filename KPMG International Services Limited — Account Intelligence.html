About KPMG International Services Limited

KPMG International Services Limited (registered in Netherlands, operational HQ Amstelveen) is the global coordinating entity for the KPMG network — 143+ member firms across ~145 countries, ~273,000 employees network-wide. Market Intelligence Highlights

Signals amplifying attrition risk

  • Governance entity, not a sales org. KPMG International coordinates the network — it has no sales pipeline, no quota-carrying reps, and no natural use for Sales Cloud's core capabilities. The $11M subscription predates any identified internal champion.
  • Microsoft-first at $2B+. KPMG committed $2B+ over 2023–2026 to Azure + OpenAI (launched internal "KymChat" tool). This directly competes with Salesforce for KPMG's innovation budget and C-suite attention.
  • Revenue growth stalling. FY2024 network revenue grew only ~3% — the weakest of the Big 4. Advisory contracted as global M&A deal volumes dropped. Cost discipline (US, UK, Australia layoffs) signals enterprise austerity.
  • Regulatory & legal pressure. £21M FRC fine (UK, 2023), US SEC settlement (2023) — financial and reputational overhang tightening discretionary technology spend.
  • No internal Salesforce champion found. Canceled Onboarding ADR (Mar 2026) has no description — the original sponsor likely departed or was never formally appointed.
  • KPMG as SI partner creates resistance. KPMG implements Salesforce for clients but uses Microsoft internally — a structurally awkward position that discourages internal adoption advocacy.

Potential re-engagement angles

  • SI practice strengthening. KPMG is a Salesforce implementation partner. Internal platform proficiency directly improves their client delivery quality and competitive positioning — a business case that bypasses the "we don't need CRM" objection.
  • Tableau bridge. KPMG already uses Tableau. A joint use case connecting Sales Cloud data to Tableau analytics is a low-friction entry point that shows value without requiring full CRM adoption.
  • Agentforce as a complement to Azure AI. Position Agentforce not as competing with KymChat/Azure, but as the client-facing AI layer for KPMG's advisory services — a different use case than internal productivity.
  • "KPMG 2030" strategy alignment. KPMG's stated pivot to audit quality and trust creates a potential angle for Salesforce as a program management and stakeholder engagement platform for large multi-jurisdictional engagements.
  • Experience Cloud for member firm coordination. KPMG International's core function is coordinating 143+ member firms globally — Salesforce Experience Cloud is purpose-built for exactly this kind of partner/network portal use case.
