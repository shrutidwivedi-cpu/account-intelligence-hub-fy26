Company Overview

BT Group PLC is the UK's largest telecommunications and network services provider, operating under brands including BT, EE (mobile), and Openreach (network infrastructure). It serves consumers, businesses, and governments across the UK and globally. BT is in the middle of a multi-year transformation: aggressively deploying full-fibre broadband (FTTP) while executing a major cost-reduction programme targeting £3bn in savings.
