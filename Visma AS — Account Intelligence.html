Company Overview

Visma AS is a Norwegian software company and one of Europe's largest providers of business software (accounting, ERP, payroll, HR) for SMEs and public sector organisations. Headquartered in Oslo, Visma owns 150+ software companies serving 2.4 million customers across Europe and Latin America. It is majority-owned by HgCapital (70.2%), a PE firm, with GIC (Singapore sovereign wealth) holding 14.2%. Visma has maintained double-digit revenue growth for over two decades through aggressive acquisition.
