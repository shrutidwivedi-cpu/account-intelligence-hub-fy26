Legal Industry CRM Context
  • Law firm BD (Business Development) is the primary Salesforce use case in legal — managing attorney relationships with clients, tracking pitches, managing events/conferences, and monitoring client interactions.
  • AI disruption in legal: Law firms are facing significant AI disruption from tools like Harvey, Clio, and CoCounsel that automate legal research, document review, and contract analysis — CIOs at large law firms are evaluating AI tools aggressively, which may crowd out traditional CRM investment priorities.
  • Alternative CRM for legal: InterAction (by LexisNexis) and Intapp are purpose-built for law firm CRM — competitors with better industry fit than Salesforce Sales Cloud for small-to-mid legal BD teams.
  • Partnership governance: LLP subscription renewals often require partnership committee approval — unlike corporate IT decisions, law firm renewals involve multiple senior stakeholders who may not use the system day-to-day.
  • Lateral hiring risk: If the original Salesforce champion attorney left for another firm, the internal renewal advocate may no longer exist.
