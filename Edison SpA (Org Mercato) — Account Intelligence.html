Company Overview

Edison S.p.A. is one of Italy's major integrated energy companies, majority-controlled by EDF (France) at 99.47% via Transalpina di Energia. Its businesses span electricity generation, natural gas procurement, renewables (2.3 GW), retail energy supply to 3.16 million customers, and energy services. Despite strong revenue, profitability has declined sharply since 2023, driven by LNG supply disruptions, low hydro generation, and retail competition.
