Company Overview

Dubai Holding Corporate LLC is the corporate entity of Dubai Holding, a UAE state-owned investment conglomerate serving as the personal investment portfolio of Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum (Ruler of Dubai). It operates across hospitality (Jumeirah Group), real estate (Dubai Properties, Meraas), financial services, media, healthcare, and business parks (TECOM Group). With assets exceeding AED 130 billion, Dubai Holding is a cornerstone of Dubai's economic diversification strategy.
