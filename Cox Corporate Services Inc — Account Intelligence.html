Company Overview

Cox Corporate Services, Inc. is the shared services entity for Cox Enterprises, a privately held Atlanta-based conglomerate with revenues of approximately $21–22bn. Cox Enterprises owns Cox Communications (cable/internet), Cox Automotive (AutoTrader, Kelley Blue Book, Manheim), and Cox Media Group (29% stake). Cox is family-owned, led by fourth-generation family CEO Alexander C. Taylor. In 2025, Cox Communications announced a merger with Charter Communications, with Cox Enterprises receiving a 23% stake in the combined entity.
