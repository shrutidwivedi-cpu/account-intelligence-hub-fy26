Company Overview

Disney Worldwide Services, Inc. is The Walt Disney Company's shared services entity, centralising HR, finance, procurement, IT, and legal functions across Disney's global operations. The Walt Disney Company (parent) is a $102bn revenue entertainment, media, and experiences conglomerate operating Disney Entertainment, Disney Experiences (theme parks), and ESPN (72% ownership). Josh D'Amaro became CEO in March 2026 following Bob Iger's planned departure.
